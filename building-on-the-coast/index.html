Building on the coast

Rain, wind, salt air and remote sites. Here’s how that changes a build, and what we do about it.

How is building on the coast different?

Heavy rain, wind and salt air call for careful detailing, rainscreen walls and materials and fasteners that stand up to the weather. Remote sites also need planning for access, deliveries and working around the seasons.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Ucluelet and build across Tofino, the West Coast, Whistler, the Sunshine Coast and remote coastal British Columbia.

How long do permits and approvals take?

It varies with the municipality or regional district, the type of project and how complete the drawings are. We help you understand what is needed and work with your designer to keep things moving.

Can you help if I don’t have a lot yet?

Yes. We can walk a lot with you before you buy and point out what affects the build, such as access, slope, rock, drainage and services.

How much does it cost to build a custom home out here?

It depends on the size of the home, the site and the finishes you choose. Building on the coast also adds remote-location costs, like getting materials and trades to site and preparing rocky or sloped lots. We give you a realistic ballpark after a site visit and a look at your plans or ideas.

How long does a build take?

It depends on the size and complexity of the home, the site and the weather. Before we start, we set out a schedule with you, then keep you updated at every stage.

Planning a build on the coast?

Tell us what you’re planning. It takes a minute, and only your name, phone, email and project type are needed to start.
