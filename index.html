How we build

Built for three metres of rain a year.

The west coast is hard on buildings. Rain comes sideways, salt gets into everything, and a small mistake in the wall becomes rot in five years. We build using building science and modern methods so your home stays dry, warm and solid for decades.

And we manage every job properly: a clear schedule, a clear budget, and regular updates so you're never guessing. Chris to confirm wording
